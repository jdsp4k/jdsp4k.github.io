Mar 2020 - Mar 2024

Lichfield Cave SSR Model

Mar 2020 - Mar 2024

This machine learning model is designed to infer subsurface spectral responses from RGB imagery in the context of medieval manuscripts. Leveraging data from the Lichfield site, the model uses supervised deep-learning techniques to predict hidden spectral bands, enabling non-invasive exploration and material identification. With an emphasis on scalability and reproducibility, the pipeline integrates preprocessing, tiling, training and inference into a streamlined research toolkit.
